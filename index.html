ABOUT ORCHID LAB

A web-based learning portal created to support the management and access of Science learning resources for Grade 8 – Orchid students of NVGCHS.

PROJECT PURPOSE

The portal provides a centralized digital space where students can access teacher-provided learning resources, view announcements, check activity information, and keep track of their learning.


Its visual identity combines science laboratory elements, neon fire-punk energy, chemistry-inspired graphics, and a galaxy-themed interface.
